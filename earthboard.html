Earthboard Basemap Style

This project was originally going to be a stylization of a basemap for the earth with the metal bismuth but instead I chose to simplify the design and go with a related amalgam of metal that is the motherboard. The points of interest will be raw metal mining operations that eventually find their procurement in electronic factories that end up becoming motherboards.
